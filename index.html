Biography

Arshad Azeez M is an Indian software entrepreneur and the founder of SidhiLynx, a technology company focused on institutional automation and engineering education systems.

He is currently pursuing undergraduate studies in electronics engineering in India and works on software systems related to academic visualization and workflow automation.

Mission

Arshad Azeez M is the founder and CEO of SidhiLynx, a technology company based in India dedicated to engineering scalable, deterministic systems for institutional and educational advancement. Under his strategic leadership, SidhiLynx has evolved into a specialized technological ecosystem designed to resolve systemic frictions through high-fidelity automation and architectural rigor.

His methodology is rooted in the principle of Empirical Hardening: the conviction that the most resilient digital solutions are forged through the direct experience of the structural challenges they intend to mitigate. This philosophy drives the development of the SidhiLynx protocol, ensuring that every software deployment is built for longevity and precision.

Beyond immediate software architecture, Arshad advocates for a future where technology acts as a stabilizing force for global institutions. By prioritizing interoperability and open-standard protocols, he aims to empower the next generation of engineers with tools like EngDraft, ensuring that digital governance remains as transparent as it is powerful.

"Our mission is to transform systemic friction into deterministic growth through code that is as resilient as the institutions it serves."
"We believe the most effective solutions are built by those who have lived the problem — not just studied it."

Technical Works

Currently pursuing a degree in Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) at Anna University (Batch 2025–2029), Arshad has independently built and shipped a broad range of full-stack software systems spanning real-time communication, media streaming, AI, and academic automation.

Proprietary Engines

DSL Flowchart Engine

A proprietary engine that translates high-level code into academic-standard flowcharts via a custom Domain Specific Language, leveraging LLM-to-DSL conversion for architectural precision. Core component of the Lumetrix platform.

CADEVEL Engine

Interprets natural language engineering graphics problems and programmatically generates step-by-step technical drafting explanations. Powers the EngDraft platform.

Communication Systems

Encrypted Real-Time Messaging Platform Flutter · Firebase

A full-featured end-to-end encrypted chat system with real-time message delivery, read receipts, and media sharing. Supports one-on-one and group conversations with persistent message history.

Media Platforms

Video Streaming Platform Flutter · Firebase

A full-stack video streaming application supporting upload, playback, channel management, and user subscriptions. Architected around Firebase for real-time metadata sync and Flutter for a cross-platform client experience.

Audio Streaming Platform Flutter · Firebase

A cross-platform audio streaming application with playlist management, artist discovery, and real-time playback controls.

AI & Automation

Instapresent — AI Attendance System Flutter · Firebase · Flask

A face recognition and AI-driven smart attendance system. Combines a Flutter mobile client with a Flask-powered inference backend and Firebase for real-time data persistence. Designed for institutional deployment with minimal manual overhead.

Mikey — Voice-First AI Chatbot Flutter · Firebase · Mistral API

An audio-native conversational AI assistant powered by the Mistral language model. Users interact entirely through voice — the system transcribes input, processes intent via Mistral, and responds with synthesized audio.

Lumetrix v1 — Automated Academic PDF Generator React · Flutter · Playwright · FastAPI · Firebase

An automated pipeline that scrapes structured data from JupyterLab environments using Playwright, injects code, captures execution output screenshots, and synthesizes a complete academic-grade PDF — including algorithm write-ups, DSL-generated flowcharts, and code blocks — with zero manual formatting.
