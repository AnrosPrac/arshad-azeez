Arshad Azeez M

Arshad Azeez M is an Indian software entrepreneur and the founder & CEO of SidhiLynx, a technology company based in Chennai. He completed his schooling with biology at Seethakkathi Matriculation Higher Secondary School, Chennai, and is now pursuing a B.Tech. in Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) at the College of Engineering, Guindy (CEG), Anna University. At SidhiLynx, he leads product and engineering, building software such as Arsaze, an AI-native video editor, and Lumetrix Play, AI-powered personalized coding paths for learners.
