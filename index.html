Founder & CEO, SidhiLynx

Arshad Azeez M

Arshad Azeez M is an Indian software entrepreneur and the founder & CEO of SidhiLynx, a technology company based in Chennai. He founded SidhiLynx in December 2025 and has led two product launches: Lumetrix Play, which has passed 750 users, and Arsaze, an AI-native video editor now used in 60 countries, featured on Product Hunt and FoundrList, and used by E-Cell, IIT Madras. He studies electronics engineering at Anna University.
